Accommodation choices

Hotels and Stays in Mongolia

Morning Ride Travel & Tours helps guests choose accommodation that fits the route, comfort level, season, and travel style.

Guest accommodation

Choose the stay that fits your journey

Accommodation in Mongolia may include city hotels, tourist ger camps, guesthouses, homestays, or camping options depending on the destination.

City Hotels

Comfortable hotels in Ulaanbaatar and provincial centers for arrival nights, city tours, business travelers, and guests who prefer standard services.

Tourist Ger Camps

The most common countryside accommodation, offering traditional ger stays with meals, shared facilities, and access to major sightseeing areas.

Guesthouses

Simple local accommodation options in towns and regional centers, useful for flexible routes, private travelers, and budget-conscious guests.

Homestays

Local family stays for travelers interested in nomadic lifestyle, hospitality, culture, and a closer countryside experience.

Ger Camp and Tent Options

Selected routes may include ger camp stays or tent camping for horse-riding, adventure, and remote countryside programs.
