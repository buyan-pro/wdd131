Explore Mongolia Tour Styles

Morning Ride Travel & Tours organizes Mongolia journeys by destination, travel style, season, comfort level, and available infrastructure.

Tour classifications

Choose the type of journey that fits your travel style

Classic Tours

Comfortable tours around Ulaanbaatar and Central Mongolia, suitable for first-time visitors, short stays, families, and stopover travelers.

Discovery Expeditions

Popular countryside routes to the Gobi, Khovsgol, Arkhangai, Ovorkhangai, and other major tourism regions with established ger camp facilities.

Adventure Explorations

Remote journeys to regions such as Bayan-Olgiy, Khentii, Dornod, and Khovd for travelers seeking culture, history, wildlife, and less-developed routes.

Horse-Riding Tours

Active Mongolia journeys designed around horseback travel, open steppe landscapes, nomadic culture, and selected riding routes for guests who want a closer experience of the countryside.
